---
name: Whole-building sale scope
description: Approved fact boundaries and change-review rule for Offered residential whole-building Sale.
---

For Offered residential whole-building Sale, building-level facts and one-apartment facts are distinct. Bedrooms and bathrooms describe one apartment, never the whole building. Total units are manually entered and do not derive from floors multiplied by apartments per typical floor. Legacy building bedroom/master-bedroom values must not be reinterpreted as apartment facts.

**Why:** The Founder approved a distinct whole-building form and explicitly limited the approved scope; misleading facts or silently inferred totals would misrepresent the property.

**How to apply:** Preserve legacy values and drafts unless the user explicitly reviews a destructive transition. Any later addition or removal of form fields remains subject to the Founder's review.
