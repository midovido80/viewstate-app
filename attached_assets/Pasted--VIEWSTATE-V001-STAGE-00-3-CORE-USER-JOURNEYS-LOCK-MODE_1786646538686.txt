# 2. FROZEN PRODUCT CONTEXT — DO NOT MODIFY

**Product**

Private, per-broker mobile tool for real-estate brokers / consultants.

Arabic + English.

Kuwait-first.

Architecture must remain suitable for future GCC expansion without turning V001 into a GCC-wide product now.

**Operating Model**

Capture → Organize → Act

**Product Principle**

Capture First → Enrich Later

**North Star**

Matching.

**Basic Capture Target**

Approximately 10–15 seconds for BASIC capture of:

- Property
- Contact
- Requirement

Optional enrichment is not required to fit inside the basic-capture target.

---

## CONTACTS

Exactly four roles:

- Tenant
- Buyer
- Owner
- Broker

Multi-role contacts are supported.

Do NOT introduce another contact role.

---

## REQUIREMENTS

A Buyer or Tenant contact may have one or more Requirements.

Each Requirement is independently:

- captured
- enriched
- retrieved
- matched

Do NOT introduce Requirement lifecycle, expiration, priority, active/inactive states, or pipeline behavior.

---

## MATCHING

Matching is bidirectional.

Direction A:

**Requirement → Matching Properties**

Direction B:

**Property → Matching Requirements / Relevant Buyer-Tenant Contacts**

Both directions follow:

**Compare → Score (0–100) → Explain**

Matching remains:

- broker-initiated
- rule-based
- private
- per-broker
- non-AI
- non-automated
- non-marketplace

Do NOT define scoring weights, threshold behavior, algorithm implementation, background matching, or notifications.

---

## CLASSIFICATION

Exactly four optional labels:

1. Follow Up
2. Important
3. Pending
4. Order Complete / Closed Deal

One classification at a time per supported record.

Classification is not Tasks, Reminders, Calendar, Automation, Pipeline, Deal Management, or Commission Management.

---

## NOTES

V001 supports:

- one editable running internal Contact Note per Contact
- one editable running internal Property Note per Property

Property Notes remain separate from Property Description.

No multiple entries, timeline, history, or task conversion.

---

## MEDIA

Photos, Videos, and Documents are supported as attachments to Property records only.

Do NOT define storage technology or file-handling implementation.
